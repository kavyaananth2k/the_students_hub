Academic Guidance

Personalised academic guidance for primary, secondary and sixth form students, alongside specialist preparation for grammar school entrance, revision planning and future pathways.

Stage-by-Stage Support

Targeted guidance across every academic stage

Key Stage 2

We support learners in Years 3 to 6 through reading comprehension, spelling, grammar, writing, number work, fractions, measurement, geometry and problem solving.

Key Stage 3

Students receive structured support across Maths, Science, English, Computing and key transition skills that prepare them for GCSE study.

GCSE & IGCSE

Our guidance includes exam board alignment, timed paper practice, performance feedback and focused revision for stronger outcomes.

AS & A2 Levels

We help students develop advanced knowledge in Maths, Sciences and other key subjects while supporting university planning and long-term goals.

Entrance Exams

11+ and 13+ preparation

Mathematics

  • Place value, number operations, fractions and percentages.
  • Ratio, measurement, geometry, algebra and problem-solving.
  • Quick mental arithmetic and exam speed drills.

English

Revision Skills

Study skills that make a difference

Active recall

Students practise retrieving knowledge from memory, which improves retention and exam confidence.

Spaced repetition

We use structured review schedules to reinforce learning over time and reduce last-minute cramming.

Time management

Students learn how to organise revision, plan effectively and build strong routines for school and examinations.
