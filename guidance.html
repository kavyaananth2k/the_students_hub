School Partnership

About Us

We are online tuition academy that strongly believes every child has talent and deserves the opportunity to develop the skills and confidence needed to achieve success—both in school and beyond—regardless of their background.

Our Mission

The Students Hub was founded by a team of committed and experienced tutors and education professionals who share a passion for improving the attainment of every child. We are especially dedicated to empowering and inspiring children from disadvantaged backgrounds, ensuring they receive the support necessary to reach their full potential.

Creating Opportunities for Every Child

We strive to create a positive and nurturing environment where every child can grow academically, emotionally, and socially. Our work aims to make a meaningful impact on each student’s:

🌟

Self-Esteem

Nurturing self-belief, emotional resilience, and a positive mindset toward learning challenges.

📈

Academic Progress

Closing curriculum attainment gaps in Maths, English, and Science with measurable grade improvements.

💼

Long-Term Economic Well-Being

Instilling lifelong critical thinking, study habits, and long-term economic well-being.

🎓

Future Success

Opening pathways to top grammar schools, high-performing sixth forms, and competitive universities.

Supporting Disadvantaged Pupils

Our primary focus is on providing personalised support for:

  • Pupils from disadvantaged backgrounds
  • Pupils eligible for Pupil Premium funding
  • Pupils with SEND
  • Pupils in Alternative Provision

We are committed to partnering with all primary and secondary schools in the area to ensure their pupils can benefit from our tailored support.

Flexible Tuition Models

To meet diverse learning needs, we offer flexible tuition models, including:

  • Live interactive online tuition
  • Small-group online sessions
  • One-to-one personalised online support
  • Digital resources & lesson recordings

We design programmes around school timetables, after-school hours, and weekend sessions.

Working With Schools & Community Organisations

As an experienced tuition provider, we have worked with various organisations such as:

📚 Local libraries 🏛️ Youth & community hubs 🏫 Schools 🤝 Educational support groups

We design bespoke programmes to meet the unique needs of each organisation, supporting both general studies and preparation for targeted examinations.

Our Commitment During and Beyond the Pandemic

We are fully prepared to work with any primary or secondary school, youth organisation, or educational charity to help children especially those from disadvantaged backgrounds recover from learning loss caused by the pandemic and continue progressing in the years ahead.

ACADEMIC GUIDANCE

Academic Guidance

Academic Guidance for Primary, Secondary & Sixth Form Students

[Primary] [Secondary] [Advanced Academic Coaching]

At The Students Hub, we provide personalised online academic support designed to help every learner grow in confidence, close attainment gaps and achieve their full potential. Our programmes are tailored to the needs of primary, secondary, and sixth-form students, ensuring the right guidance at every stage of their educational journey.

Primary Level

Primary School Support (KS1–KS2)

📖 Building Strong Foundations

We help younger learners develop essential literacy and numeracy skills, supporting:

  • Reading fluency, phonics & comprehension
  • Core maths skills including number bonds, place value & times tables
  • Early writing skills (spelling, punctuation, vocabulary)
🌱 Confidence & Learning Habits

We nurture curiosity, motivation and independence through:

  • Positive learning habits
  • Growth mindset and resilience
  • Encouraging problem-solving skills
🎯 SATs & 11+ Preparation

Specialist support for:

  • Year 2 & Year 6 SATs
  • 11+ exams (GL & CEM) in verbal, non-verbal reasoning, English & maths
Secondary Level

Secondary School Support (KS3–KS4 / GCSE)

📐 Subject Mastery & Skill Development

We provide targeted academic support across:

  • Maths, English & Science
  • Humanities, Languages & Computing
  • Foundation-building for KS3 transition and GCSE success
🧠 Effective Study & Revision Skills

Students learn how to revise smarter, not harder:

  • Active recall & spaced repetition
  • Time management & organisation
  • Understanding exam command words
  • Past papers, model answers & exam technique coaching
📝 GCSE Exam Preparation & Careers

We offer comprehensive preparation for AQA, Edexcel and OCR subjects including:

  • Topic-by-topic teaching
  • Mock exams and feedback
  • Exam strategy and confidence-building
  • Careers Awareness: Students receive early guidance on post-16 options and how GCSE choices shape future opportunities.
Sixth Form Level

Sixth Form Support (A-Level / BTEC / Vocational Pathways)

🔬 Advanced Academic Coaching

We offer tailored sessions in key A-Level subjects such as:

  • Maths, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Psychology, Sociology, Business, Economics
  • English Literature, History, Geography & more
📖 Independent Study & Academic Success

We help students adapt to the demands of advanced study:

  • Critical thinking and extended writing
  • Managing deadlines and workload
  • Advanced revision & exam strategies
🎓 University, Apprenticeship & Future-Ready Skills

Our progression support includes:

  • UCAS personal statements
  • Course selection & interview preparation
  • Apprenticeship and vocational pathway advice
  • CV development and employability skills
  • Communication & presentation
  • Research, digital literacy, and academic referencing

Helping Every Learner Thrive

Our mission is to make learning accessible, enjoyable, and effective for every student. Whether your child needs additional academic support, confidence building, or structured exam preparation, we’re here to help them move forward with clarity and confidence.

REVISION WORKSHOPS

Revision Workshops

Exam Revision & Booster Sessions

The Students Hub offers exclusive online revision sessions designed to strengthen students’ final exam preparation. These targeted sessions are available before and during exam periods, as well as through intensive programmes held during the Easter and Summer holidays.

Comprehensive Booster Courses

Our booster sessions include crash courses that thoroughly cover the entire syllabus. These sessions are ideal for students preparing for:

  • GCSE
  • IGCSE
  • A Levels
  • 11 Plus
  • 13 Plus

These courses focus on revisiting missed or challenging topics, reinforcing key concepts, and ensuring students have a solid understanding of all subject areas.

Focused Exam Preparation

We prioritise hands-on, exam-focused learning by:

  • Practising a wide range of exam-style questions
  • Providing abundant opportunities to solve past papers
  • Strengthening exam technique and confidence
  • Offering personalised support to close learning gaps

Our goal is to equip every student with the skills, knowledge, and confidence they need to achieve their aspirational grades.
