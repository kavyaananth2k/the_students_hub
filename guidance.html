Stage-by-Stage Support

Academic Guidance for Primary, Secondary & Sixth Form

At our tuition centre, we provide personalised academic support designed to help every learner grow in confidence, close attainment gaps, and achieve their full potential.

Years 1 – 6

🎒 Primary School Support (KS1–KS2)

Building Strong Foundations:

We help younger learners develop essential literacy and numeracy skills: reading fluency, phonics, comprehension, core maths (number bonds, place value, times tables), and early writing skills (spelling, punctuation, vocabulary).

Confidence & Learning Habits:

Nurturing curiosity, motivation, and independence through positive learning habits, growth mindset, resilience, and problem-solving skills.

SATs & 11+ Preparation:

Specialist support for Year 2 & Year 6 SATs, plus 11+ entrance exams (GL & CEM) in Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, English, and Mathematics.

Years 7 – 11

📐 Secondary Support (KS3–KS4 / GCSE)

Subject Mastery & Foundations:

Targeted academic support across Maths, English, Science, Humanities, Languages, and Computing. Smooth foundation building for the KS3 transition and GCSE success.

Effective Study & Revision Skills:

Students learn how to revise smarter, not harder: active recall, spaced repetition, time management, organisation, and decoding exam command words.

GCSE Exam Preparation & Careers:

Comprehensive preparation for AQA, Edexcel, and OCR boards, mock exams with feedback, and early guidance on post-16 options and career choices.

Years 12 – 13

🏛️ Sixth Form Coaching & UCAS

Advanced Academic Coaching:

Tailored sessions in key A-Level subjects: Maths, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Psychology, Sociology, and English Literature.

Independent Study & Success:

Critical thinking, extended essay writing, managing deadlines, advanced exam strategies, and future-ready digital and AI-assisted learning literacy.

University & Apprenticeships:

UCAS personal statements, university course selection, interview preparation, apprenticeship advice, and CV development.
