Cyber Security Essentials

Cyber Security Essentials & Be Safe Online
Ages 9–16 (Adaptable for Primary or Lower Secondary)
30–40 Hours (10 Modules, 3–4 Hours Each)

🎯 Course Overview

This course helps children understand how to stay safe in the digital world, protect their personal information, and make smart choices online. Through interactive activities, they will learn about cyber threats, online etiquette, password security, social media safety, and digital wellbeing.

By the end of the course, students will be able to recognise online risks and apply strategies to protect themselves and others from cyber dangers.

🧠 Learning Outcomes

1. Understand what cybersecurity means and why it’s important.
2. Recognise and avoid online risks like scams, phishing, and malware.
3. Create strong passwords and secure online accounts.
4. Use technology responsibly on social media, games, and devices.
5. Identify fake news, cyberbullying, and online manipulation.
6. Practise safe online habits to protect privacy and digital reputation.

🛠️ Tools & Requirements

Platforms: Google Interland (Be Internet Awesome), CyberFirst (for teens), Be Internet Legends (younger kids)

Software/Demos: Canva (for posters), Kahoot! (for quizzes), Scratch or HTML for mini-cyber projects

Supplementary: Printable worksheets, role-play cards, and case studies
