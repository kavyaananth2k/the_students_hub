Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining The Students Hub.

Every child completes our 45-minute baseline diagnostic assessment. This isn't a pass/fail test; it maps out curriculum competencies and flags specific blind spots. Following the assessment, our coordinator provides a full breakdown of their working grade and recommended roadmap.
We cap our small groups at a maximum of 6 students. This guarantees that each student receives individual question time and tailored guidance while benefiting from the motivation and collaborative energy of fellow ambitious peers.
Yes! We offer bespoke 1-to-1 private tutoring across all levels (11+, KS2/3, GCSE, and A-Levels) both at our centre and live online. This is ideal for students who want hyper-accelerated preparation or need intensive support in specific topics.
For 11+ and GCSE cohorts, we conduct bi-weekly timed mock exams starting 6 months before national examinations. Mock papers are marked using official examination mark schemes, accompanied by question-by-question diagnostic error logs.
