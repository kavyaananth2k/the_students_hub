Verbal Reasoning

11+ Verbal Reasoning

GL • CEM • Independent
Logic & Reasoning Topics

These are logic / reasoning questions using words. Not part of the national curriculum, so schools and exam boards vary.

  • Analogies (word analogies)
  • Opposites / antonyms
  • Synonyms
  • Word meanings, vocabulary in context
  • Cloze texts (fill in missing words)
  • Codes & code words (simple ciphers)
  • Hidden words / missing letters
  • Word puzzles: making words from letters, rearrangements (anagrams)
  • Letter series / number-letter codes
  • Word-number connections (e.g. letters represent numbers etc.)
  • Following instructions with words, logical word problem statements (Education Quizzes)

11+ Non-Verbal Reasoning

Spatial Logic • Visual Patterns
Spatial, Pattern & Visual Logic

These test spatial, pattern, and visual logic skills. More variable between exam boards / regions.

  • Some areas include numerical reasoning, separate from maths or VR.

13+ Entrance

Examination Modules

ALL SUBJECTS MATHS ENGLISH VERBAL REASONING NON-VERBAL REASONING
Mathematics

13+ Mathematics

ISEB CE Maths Syllabus
Components / Structure
  • Three papers: Non-calculator written paper, Calculator paper, and Mental arithmetic (short timed test).
  • Levels: Foundation, Core, and Additional (Higher / more challenging for more able pupils).
1. Number
  • Place value (integers, decimals), ordering, rounding.
  • Negative numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages.
  • Prime numbers; factors; multiples; highest common factor (HCF), lowest common multiple (LCM); prime factorisation.
2. Algebra
  • Expressions, equations, inequalities.
  • Sequences, nth term.
  • Manipulation like expanding, factorising.
  • Substitution into formulae.
3. Ratio, Proportion & Rates of Change
  • Ratios, proportional reasoning.
  • Scaling, maps, diagrams.
4. Geometry & Measures
  • Properties of shapes (2D & 3D), classification, angles, lines, polygons.
  • Perimeter, area, volume (including of prisms, cylinders).
  • Transformations: reflection, rotation, translation, enlargement / scale factor.
  • Coordinates; measurement units; conversion; time, speed, distance etc.
5. Statistics & Probability
  • Data handling: mean, median, mode, range.
  • Probability basics.

English

13+ English (Reading & Writing)

ISEB CE English Syllabus
Components / Structure
  • Two papers: One “Reading” (comprehension & analysis) and one “Writing”.
  • Levels: Usually “Foundation” (easier) or “Core” (standard).
  • Extracts: Prose, poetry, drama. May include unseen passages.
Skills / Topics Tested
  • Reading comprehension: literal understanding, inference, summarising, comparing texts.
  • Analysis of language: how grammar, syntax, punctuation, literary/trope devices affect meaning (e.g. metaphor, simile, imagery, alliteration etc.).
  • Vocabulary in context, meaning of words, shades of meaning.
  • Writing styles/formats: narrative, descriptive, persuasive, argumentative, letter, report, essay etc.
  • Grammar, punctuation, spelling accuracy, register and tone.

Verbal Reasoning in 13+

VERBAL REASONING is not always compulsory in CE 13+, unlike some 11+ grammar exams. When used (pre-tests or specific schools), topics typically include:

  • Synonyms, antonyms.
  • Analogies.
  • Word codes, letter series.
  • Following instructions, word puzzles.
  • Cloze tests (fill in missing words).
