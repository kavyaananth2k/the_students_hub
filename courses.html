Complete Academic Tuition Syllabi

Detailed topic breakdowns taught across Key Stage 2, Key Stage 3, GCSE, and A-Levels as documented in our official academy syllabus.

Years 3 to 6

Key Stage 2 (Years 3 – 6)

Mathematics:

Place value (up to 10M), 4 operations with formal written algorithms, fractions, decimals & percentages, ratio/proportion, introductory algebra & sequences, perimeter/area/volume, 2D/3D geometry, angles in polygons, and statistics (line graphs, pie charts, mean).

English:

Comprehension, inference, summarising themes, figurative language, balanced arguments, narratives with suspense & flashbacks, SPaG (fronted adverbials, relative clauses, passive voice, colons, semicolons), and statutory spelling words.

Years 7 to 9

Key Stage 3 (Years 7 – 9)

Maths & Computing:

BIDMAS, standard form, expanding/factorising linear equations, Pythagoras theorem, circle theorems, transformations, probability, hardware/software, binary representation, and Python algorithms.

English & Science:

Shakespeare, 19th-century prose, analytical writing (PEE/PEEL), poetry comparisons, alongside Science: Cells, digestion, respiration, photosynthesis, periodic table, reactions, forces, energy transfers, sound & light.

Entrance Exams

11+ & 13+ Entrance Exams

VR & Non-Verbal Reasoning:

Codes, analogies, hidden words, compound words, antonyms/synonyms, alphabet series, 3D cube nets, spatial rotation, reflection, matrices, odd-one-out, and layered patterns.

Maths & English (GL & CEM):

Rapid mental arithmetic, algebra, word problems, high-yield vocabulary, cloze tests, comprehension, and creative/persuasive writing mark scheme criteria.

Years 10 – 13

GCSE, IGCSE & A-Levels

GCSE Higher (Grades 8 & 9):

Higher algebraic proofs, vectors, quadratic formula, circle theorems, organic chemistry, electrolysis, bioenergetics, genetics, electromagnetic waves, and English Literature texts.

AS & A2 Level Mastery:

Pure Maths (calculus, trigonometry, proof), Further Maths (matrices, complex numbers), Biology (gene expression, ecology), Chemistry (spectroscopy, NMR, kinetics, thermodynamics), and Physics (fields, nuclear, mechanics).
