Academic Tuition Programmes

From early foundational learning to GCSE, IGCSE, A-Level study and coding/STEM enrichment, our approach is designed to help students become confident, capable and fully prepared for future success.

Key Stage 2

Years 3 to 6

Year 3

  • Maths: place value, fractions, measurement, geometry and statistics.
  • English: comprehension, inference, narrative and grammar.

Year 4

  • Maths: 4-digit operations, fractions, decimals, area and perimeter.
  • English: persuasive writing, diary entries, grammar and speaking.

Year 5

  • Maths: multi-step arithmetic, long multiplication and division, geometry and volume.
  • English: advanced comprehension, figurative language and formal presentations.

Year 6

  • Maths: ratio, algebra, geometry, area, perimeter and statistics.
  • English: balanced arguments, narrative techniques, formal reporting and debate.
Key Stage 3

Years 7 to 9

Year 7

  • Maths: number, algebra, geometry, data handling and ratio basics.
  • Science: biology, chemistry and physics foundations.
  • Computer Science: hardware, software, algorithms and e-safety.

Year 8

  • Maths: standard form, equations, sequences and statistics.
  • Science: ecosystems, periodic table, electricity and renewable energy.
  • Computer Science: Python, networks, cybersecurity and spreadsheets.

Year 9

  • Maths: indices, surds, proportion, quadratics and graphs.
  • Science: inheritance, atomic structure and particle model themes.
  • Computer Science: advanced Python, logic gates, data representation and GCSE preparation.
GCSE & IGCSE

Years 10 and 11

Maths

Fractions, decimals, percentages, algebra, quadratics, trigonometry, circle theorems and statistics.

English

Analytical writing, literature responses and exam-style practice across language and literature.

Science

Biology, chemistry and physics support with required practicals, quantitative chemistry and conceptual understanding.

Computer Science

Programming, algorithms, data representation and computing problem-solving to support exam confidence.

AS & A2 Levels

Years 12 and 13

Mathematics & Further Maths

Calculus, integration, complex numbers, matrices, differential equations and mechanics.

Biology, Chemistry & Physics

Advanced conceptual support, experiment preparation and subject mastery for ambitious university pathways.

University Preparation

Guidance that helps students with subject choices, personal statements and long-term academic planning.
